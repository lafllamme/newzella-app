# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: plain static HTML/CSS/JS for the design exploration; framework selection deferred until a visual direction is approved.

## Users

Primary users are local customers in Köln, Leverkusen and Pulheim who want to discover fresh bakery and confectionery products, find a nearby shop, check opening information, or enquire about cakes and specialities. Secondary users include prospective employees and trainees.

## Product Purpose

Newzella presents a family-run bakery and confectionery business, its products, shops, stories, news, career opportunities and community engagement. Success means that visitors quickly understand the offer, feel appetite and trust, and can find the right shop or next action.

## Positioning

Newzella combines daily bakery and confectionery craft, local neighbourhood presence and family history since 1896 across Köln, Leverkusen and Pulheim.

## Operating Context

The website is used mostly by people planning an in-person visit from a phone or desktop. The primary rituals are choosing bread or pastries, finding a Fachgeschäft, planning a cake order, reading current notices, and learning about the people behind the business.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Content must remain grounded in the existing crawl and live site.
- Products, shops, opening hours, news and job information need to stay editable for a later CMS/framework implementation.
- The current exploration stays in HTML to reduce technical overhead.
- Online ordering, live inventory and payment are not confirmed and must not be invented.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Newzella / Bäckerei Konditorei Magnus Newzella.
- Existing logo must remain available as a core asset.
- Primärgelb `#FFE123` and Burgunder `#8B0E13` are fixed brand anchors.
- The new surface should feel modern, rounded, warm, professional and bakery-specific.
- Typography is open for visual testing; a characterful script/display face may be used for selected headlines, with calm readable text and CTA typography.

## Product Principles

- Make the daily bakery ritual immediately understandable.
- Put real products, people and local shops before abstract brand language.
- Preserve the warmth of family craft while making the experience contemporary.
- Give every major visitor intent a clear next step.
- Keep factual content and future CMS boundaries visible in the design.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use readable contrast, keyboard-visible focus, large touch targets, meaningful image alternatives, responsive layouts and reduced-motion support. Do not rely on colour alone for meaning.
